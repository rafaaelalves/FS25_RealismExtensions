# Superseded 500→540 compatibility experiment (2026-10-09)

The 500→540 mappings for Hardi AEON 5200 and Berthoud Vantage 4300 in commit `eff4f229` were based on MoreRealistic 0.26.10.08 source data, **not independently confirmed as the nominal drive family of the exact in-game vehicles**. They have been **removed** from this branch. The field `powerConsumer.ptoRpm` reports a game/MR power demand, not by itself an external gearbox specification. Additional John Deere R732i/R975i runtime examples report 340 and 400. Equipment can have a visible PTO cardan without a verified exact gearbox family.

New resolver: explicit native input PTO or verified profile is necessary for PTO-consumer detection; input with raw demand of **540/1000** permits a provisional nominal gear-family inference, and other raw demand values stay **UNKNOWN**. No raw-demand rounding, no automatic AI shift, no red mismatch for unknowns; MR load unchanged. True 540/1000 known family mismatch still warns. For raw demand conflict, do not infer physical gear conflict unless *evidenced families* differ. The inspector in this branch collects runtime evidence rather than guessing configurations.

Further OEM machine-option verification remains pending. A generic 500→540 profile is not an acceptable substitute for that verification.
