## 1. What it actually does

- The module `scripts/MudOnRoad.lua` observes all vehicles with wheels during its mission update. It reads native field-ground density **read-only**, camera/terrain height, the vehicle's real `Washable` tire mud-mesh dirt amount and, as fallback, generic tire dirt above **0.85**. It requires travel from *worked* field ground (not grass), realistic wheel-to-ground contact, then deposits roadside streaks and clods as **cloned visual I3D nodes**. Its residue on a tire declines exponentially with distance, `carryDistance=150 m`, with a max distance since leaving a worked field of **600 m** (adjusted by workload).
- Streaks bend through corners; clods fly further at speed. There is independent local airborne mud; heavy rain removes road mud in about **5 in-game hours**, and dry-weather visual lifetime defaults to **72 in-game hours**. New wet weather can darken previously dried mud.
- Existing roadside material layers are visual meshes: patch lifted ~**3–16 mm** for z-fighting/layers, not heightmap displacement or tire-physics collision; its `data/mudOnRoad.i3d` declares **no collision attributes**. No `setTerrainHeightAtWorldPos`, `TerrainDeformation`, `DensityMapModifier` or `FSDensityMapUtil.updateSprayArea` writes. Ground-type read via `FieldDensityMap.GROUND_TYPE` is classification, not a mutation. **It cannot repair RE target-vs-actual deformation overshoot or terrain clipping**.
- `scripts/BucketBrush.lua` registers a dedicated specialization on a telehandler-style `shovelLeveler` with physical looking brush rollers, gravitational swing on bucket tip, 180-rpm default roller, spin-up, sound and spray, plus alternative attachment fittings and hydraulic hose **visuals**. It finds surface contact by downward raycast, sweeps ~2.7m width, drops visually loosened mud into a 2,640L bucket of supported fill type. **There is no `PowerConsumer`, PTO demand, hydraulic pressure/load or actual motor-energy draw** for the brush; the roller rotation is directly driven by `getIsTurnedOn()` and a fixed RPM. It is a workflow/gameplay actuator, not a validated hydro-mechanical simulation.
- Supports other sweepers by active `SWEEP` workArea heuristics, and ordinary shovels scraping. The native-looking road mud sign costs 25 and exempted road dirt inside **200 m** from fines.
- **v3.0 map feature:** rasterizes roadside dirt into up to **2,000 small PDA/mini-map hotspots** (`mapSpacing=8 m`), roads-only by default, with public/fine threat and soil color. Farm fines default ON: 3 game-hour grace, at least 30 patches, periodic checks and up to 2,000 game currency each check. An admin can disable fines. Four soil color variants. Optional traffic pickup/redistribution `trackMud=false` by default.
- Server owns patch placement, aging, cleanup, fines and savegame `mudOnRoad.xml`; patches are sent in capped 250-per-message batches and full late-join snapshots; flying particles remain client-local. Network record has explicit ID and compressed visual dimensions/orientation; `MudOnRoadEvent:run` accepts server-origin events only. This is **stronger provenance than ad-hoc global client traces**, but no real dedicated server test. `modSettings/FS25_MudOnRoad.xml` is profile-global and schema version 18 **replaces older settings with new defaults** if older; save/backup first if updating from an old release.

## 2. "Brush" source learning relevant to RE

**Not native GIANTS terrain brush.** The bucket implements collision/raycast physical cleaning, while the road trace renderer does useful **read-only terrain profiling**:

- `isHardFlatGround` samples terrain **5 points along × 3 across** the intended streak footprint, and takes both native ground attributes and 15 terrain-height samples. It fits a local least-squares **tilted plane** `h=a+b*u+c*v`. It rejects any sample with a residual greater than **`flatTolerance=0.012 m`** or ground attributes indicating non-hard surface, and draws a thin streak only on sufficiently smooth hard roads. Grass/rough ground still gets clods, avoiding visually hovering "planks".
- **Reproduced source-executed Lua test:** a synthetic sloped flat plane with height `0.05x + 0.02z` accepted a streak; a synthetic -5cm center depression failed the 12mm residual threshold. This is a test of the function's numerical control flow under mocked `getTerrainAttributesAtWorldPos`, **not proof the exact GIANTS terrain attributes classify every map road correctly**.
- **RE design precedent:** a slope-invariant, multi-sample *residual* can distinguish natural incline from irregular rut geometry in a read-only observation layer. It could enrich our post-commit rut **visual diagnostics**, collision discrepancy investigations, and robust wheel-footprint classification. **It does not solve our native heightmap requested-vs-applied delta or mutation causal attribution**; a fitting algorithm must be paired with native pre/post height reads and actual authoritatively committed terrain writer output.
- Visual patch life as an external append-only/spatial-indexed `2m` cell layer, serviceable erasure with workArea geometry, server-to-client delta batches, map overlays and bounded TTL could inform **RE UI/telemetry and road dirt extension** later. Do **not copy upstream models, assets or Lua** absent permission or a suitable redistribution grant.

## 4. Concrete source-level findings and performance questions

**MOR300-01 MEDIUM — dual wheel-washable owner**. Exact source \`MudOnRoad.updateWheel\` sets \`mudNode.dirtAmount *= exp(-distance/carryDistance)\` (or adjusts fallback) on server, while exact Mud 1.4 `WheelDirtAddon` still controls wheel dirt/clod amounts by its own frame callbacks. This is **the only immediate potential RC bridge scope** but first measure 0→150m→300m drop with Mud on/off and wheel-mesh node values. Do not create a compensating multiplier or a new emitter ownership system before a reproducible conflict.

**MOR300-02 MEDIUM — source wetness is globally sampled.** At the same ground point Mud may report local wetness 0.9 while vanilla reports 0.1; the visual road factor will use the latter, even if the actual Mud wheel mesh is dirty. Not a bug in road physics (none exists), but possible missing fidelity. A later narrow client/server-consistent read-only wetness provider would be warranted only after an actual inconsistency.

**MOR300-03 MEDIUM — no hydraulic power, and nominal mud liters do not equal preserved terrain mass.** Brush can rotate at fixed RPM even without an authored `PowerConsumer` / motor-load contract. Individual patch liters are assigned by geometry, not deducted from a farm's real moved dirt/volume. There is no **geometry or physical rut recovery** when cleaning roads. A brush/full bucket does respect capacity in source; false realism claims need avoiding.

**MOR300-04 MEDIUM — potentially costly per-wheel raycasts and per-patch sweeps.** Every active wheel is processed each frame for moving vehicles and raycast-tested; source caps at **4,000 smear + 2,500 clod** nodes, up to 200 flying clods, 2,000 map markers, and scans the active vehicles every 100ms for scrubbers. `BucketBrush.collectMud` iterates the **entire patch list** every ~100ms when on ground; ordinary bucket scrape and map hotspot rebuilding also scan scene patches. 6,500 nodes plus multiple sweepers and 4× maps may have a measurable main-thread cost. No frame time numbers from source alone. Prefer performance A/B after users' current RE terrain profiling is steady; do not over-interpret `maxSmears` as typical active count.

**MOR300-05 MEDIUM — visual configuration and source bundle hygiene.** The bundled `scripts/MudOnRoad.lua.orig` (~101KB) is unreferenced by the live manifest; not executed in the normal FS25 script list but can confuse independent source audits. Current v3 descVersion 96 is older than some current FS25 mod conventions; it is an author packaging detail, **not proof of a game load failure**. Original model/script source rights are not clearly licensed in uploaded ZIP; avoid copying.

**MOR300-06 LOW/MEDIUM — input and save state concerns.** \`mudOnRoad.xml\` serializes every visual patch (coords, shape, color, farm, road age) and clients receive server delta/snapshot. Map rebuilds rely on current native road topology while saved patches replay at earlier world coordinates; changes to roads/landscaping may yield floating/buried old visuals. \`SETTINGS_VERSION=18\` refuses old schema values and writes defaults; back up prior settings when upgrading, avoid assuming exact user preferences persist. No MP correctness or terrain-after-landscape reconciliation proof from source.

## 6. Install decision and minimum A/B

**Worth trying if the user wants realistic field-to-road dirt, road cleanup operations and fines.** It adds unique road-surface *visual contamination and maintenance* that the Mud 1.4 terrain/puddle module does not own. Keep Mud 1.4 as the actual tire/sink/wetness authority, keep RE as native heightmap authority; Mud On Road may remain a cosmetic/gameplay consumer. No need to disable the entire Mud, MR, Reifen, RMS or RE to trial it — choose a **cloned save** and isolate one variable.

Minimal 10–15 minute scenario on a single known paved road leaving a worked wet field:
- Use same tractor+tires and compare **Mud On Road OFF vs ON**, with **Mud 1.4 ON**. Record tire mud mesh dirt at field gate, after 150m/300m, when parked and on return to field; check whether clods and shader dirt continue to match Mud's WheelDirtAddon.
- Inspect road smears on sloped asphalt, road with RE rut/roughness, and paved yard; whether classifying hard ground works on the actual map. Confirm simple cleanup and bucket ability to pick up supported fill type; optionally try included 180rpm brush and tipping.
- For a first compatibility run, set **fines OFF**, **nearWheelMud OFF** (disables additional dirt boost but **does not prevent on-load washable-node regrouping**), **trackMud OFF** (already default) and leave all mud deposition other than the new road visuals enabled. Re-enable those features individually after normal operation proven.
- Verify save/reload persistence and \`mudOnRoadInfo\` patch counts, visually inspect PDA hotspots. Compare FPS / RE terrain profiler when patch count grows; do not rerun complete harvest/seed/recovery cycles just to test this visual mod.

**Potential small future RC bridge** only if a clear Mud v1.4 vs Mud On Road over-cleaning or wetness mismatch is reproduced: read-only wheel mud identity / visual carry demand and scope-limited ownership to *one* setter; no dual wheel dirt writer, no inflation of physical sink, and no copying the external mod.
