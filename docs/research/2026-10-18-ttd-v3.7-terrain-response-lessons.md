## Why we investigated it

TTD simulates ground-associated vehicle response by monitoring wheel surface, global moisture, relief, chassis type, tires and implements. It also calibrates coefficients per tractor. The apparent similarities to our target — distinct surface materials, tracked/wheeled chassis, wheelspin, wet fields, tractor-trailer resistance and “ruts” — make it an instructive **counterexample in ownership design**.

Our objective, however, is to repair a **specific proven RE problem**: in recent R1/R7 terrain logs, center-height changes were frequently no-ops; structural recovery was not reliably reconstructing deep field ruts. Existing V3 research is investigating the actual GIANTS heightmap vertex resolution/target brush footprint, first-touch baseline provenance and measured feedback. Nothing in the TTD ZIP repairs those constraints.

## What TTD really does (implementation-derived)

- `TTD_StateManager` samples contact under each wheel and aggregates **field/road/natural/object** ratios, with delayed transitions. It may be a model of what data is useful, but its `FIELD_THRESHOLD_OFF` constant is not used to implement a two-threshold Schmitt hysteresis.
- `TTD_Shared.HeightCache` samples center world-height plus four cardinal probes at 3–5m and calls any higher neighbors a **relative depression** (lower neighbors: hill), caching near the wheel location for ~500ms. This **does not identify a rut baseline** and can mistake slopes/curvature/berms for anomalies. Future RE test cases should deliberately demonstrate these false positives.
- `TTD_WheelSinkSystem` reduces **`wheel.physics.radius`** and uses per-wheel transient `_ttdDigSink` when wheels struggle. It invokes `setWheelShapeTerrainDisplacement` under wheel-shape eligibility. It has **no `TerrainDeformation` or `setTerrainHeight` source call, no historical original rut vertex, no surface reconstruction**, no structural soil mass conservation. The visual/physical notion of wheel sink is not equivalent to a persisted soil cavity.
- `TTD_NaturalSystem`, `TTD_SlipSystem` and `TTD_TrailerResistance` modify `frictionScale` and `rollingResistance` on wheel physics and some engine fields. These are exactly the physical authorities of MR+Mud+Reifen+RMS, not RE. TTD's scalar trailer resistance/slip is not a canonical drawbar-force measurement.
- `TTD_Profile` and calibration UI permit per-vehicle response edits with XML persistence. As implemented, multiplayer `TTDProfileChangeEvent` addresses **role rather than stable vehicle ID** and checks `TTD_Profile.ROLE_PROFILES`, which is not created in the supplied ZIP; the event can return without applying the profile. `TTDSettingsEvent` also appears to lack sender permission validation on the server. We should borrow only the concept, not these implementations.

## Candidate independent RE improvements

### 1. Read-only per-wheel SurfaceContext (P1, valuable but *not* a new writer)

Build a research spec for a **normalized provenance-aware surface observation** (if existing `TerrainResponseModel` and RC state provider do not already fully cover it):
- Observations: wheel support/contact, GIANTS density/material/contact class, Mud's **authoritative local effective wetness**, soil treatment state, wheel/tire contact area and structural radius from current Mud/Reifen+RC owner, slip via MR/RC, actual surface elevation and optional validated pre-deformation anchors from RE.
- Each field has **source identity, units, timestamp, epoch and trust/availability**. No second “moisture,” rolling resistance, grip, sink or engine load writer.
- Derive `field/road/natural/object/mixed` state with **separate enter/exit thresholds**, short recovery debounce and explicit changes after TerraFarm edits and vehicle module updates.
- Primary consumer is **RE-owned persistent terrain geometry** and diagnostic UI; no alteration to physical wheel reaction without an additional ownership/design decision.
- **Do not implement** before comparing with currently available `ExtensionsStateProvider` and `TerrainResponseModel`. Reuse their observations instead of parallel caches.

### 2. Robust relief and wrong-reference adversarial harness (P1, direct relation to current blocker)

Extend V3 height reconstruction tests to include **real map / heightmap-aware** cases:
1. pristine plane and sloped plane (should never generate a positive “rut debt”);
2. natural convex/concave rolling land (should not be treated as a clean planar pre-rut reference);
3. native TerraFarm pit/berm with no RE history (must not silently “recover” TerraFarm edits);
4. recent RE rut with authenticated **pre-rut native heightmap vertex reference**;
5. long-lived old save with no original anchor (must retain uncertainty/avoid mounding);
6. field/grass/road boundary with mixed wheel contact and a rut traveling across;
7. moving tracked harvester vs stationary wheelspin and active cultivator no-rut-writing interlock.

Instrument **true native mesh vertex spacing**, brush footprint, before/after measured height at relevant vertices and actual TARGET completions, and V2 reference rejections by unique spatial key. Baseline measurements should be accepted only when provenance supports them. Do not import TTD's simplistic “pothole” classifier as repair intent.

### 3. Per-vehicle RE-owned calibration in a test-lab mode (P2)

Potential design:
- Profiles are **optional and versioned**, keyed by stable vehicle ID, validated server-side and persisted with forward-compatible schema. Manual, AI and multiplayer identities remain coherent.
- Scope only parameters RE actually owns: geometry response *bounds* in controlled study and native PTO operator control where already owned. Never expose fake “better grip” or Mud/Reifen radius overrides as RE options.
- Presets classify tractor, tracked harvester, loaded trailer and implements from **measured physical geometry and mass** rather than TTD's arbitrary default front axle 10% reduction or tracks `DIG_MULT=0.3`.
- UI must show **observed vs target** geometry changes, source confidence, and allow safe reset. No automatic release-mode tuning layer until calibrated against experimental evidence.
- Validate access control, not just clickable UI; the TTD MP per-role profile bug and weak settings event are negative test fixtures.

### 4. Implement/trailer ground reaction study (P3)

TTD responds to trailer mass and soft ground and tries to transfer effects to a tractor, but does so by wheel friction/rolling and a dimensionless `trailerResistanceEffect` added to slip. This is **not** the actual hitch longitudinal force in N, trailer wheel rolling torque, or engine kW. A believable RE/RC proposal should first audit GIANTS hitch physics, existing MR `PowerConsumer` draft and forces/rolling model with a loaded trailer, and decide whether **any** unowned phenomenon exists. No coding now.

### 5. Tracked wheelshape displacement research (P3)

TTD uses the GIANTS wheelshape displacement helper and track-specific multipliers; the visual rut from native GIANTS wheel displacement may sometimes appear effective without permanent terrain geometry debt. Before modifying RE:
- differentiate **native temporary wheel displacement/visual track** from true saved heightmap changes, AI tracks, TerraFarm material/deformation and RE-owned persistent geometry;
- compare stationary tracked wheelspin and moving tracked vehicles; record actual terrain height before/after and reload. Claim structural deformation only on measured native persisted height differences.
- treat TTD as a source for **experiment design**, not a source of truth about GIANTS mesh ownership.

## Acceptance rules for future research

- A new RE implementation is allowed only after it fills a specific **unowned** behavior, passes explicit owner/units analysis and is validated with comparison to source current RC/RE code.
- “Works” is measured from **gameplay geometry and real wheel response**, not just static compilation or HUD numbers.
- Do not copy third-party Lua, textures, sounds or formulations; exact ZIP includes no reusable license and public mod page has no immutable binary source digest.
- Separate **terrain structural reconstruction** from **vehicle mechanical response** and **visual effects** throughout tests.
- Keep the actual V3 terrain recovery reconstruction as the main priority; TTD observations are supporting research, not a new sprint that delays finishing the existing work.

**Research outcome:** PRIORITIZE a robust per-wheel surface context and curvature-false-positive *test architecture*; PARK calibration UI and trailer force research; RE code unchanged.
